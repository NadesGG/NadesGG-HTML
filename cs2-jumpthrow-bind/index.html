CS2 jumpthrow bind

The old bind stopped working in August 2024. Here's what changed, how to jumpthrow by hand, and how to switch the bind back on for offline practice.

Updated September 14, 2026
Quick answer

The jumpthrow bind doesn't work on Valve servers. Since the August 19, 2024 update, CS2 ignores any bind that combines more than one movement or attack action, and Valve names jumpthrow binds as one of them. Valve made jumpthrows reliable by hand instead: hold the throw button, press jump, release just after.

How to jumpthrow in CS2

  1. Pull the pin. Hold Mouse1 for a full throw, Mouse2 for a short lob, or both for a medium throw. Use whichever the lineup calls for.
  2. Line up your aim on the lineup's reference point.
  3. Press jump, and release the throw button just after.
  4. Listen for the grunt. Since March 2023, CS2 plays a short grunt only when a grenade is jump-thrown correctly. Since August 2024, nearby players hear it too. No grunt means your timing was off.

CS2 records the exact moment you release (Valve calls this subtick), so the throw doesn't depend on which server tick you hit the way it did in CS:GO. Valve's May 18, 2026 update improved jumpthrow consistency again.

A legal helper: jump on the mouse wheel

A bind that does one action is still allowed. Many players put jump on the scroll wheel, which makes the timing easier to repeat:

Console
bind "mwheeldown" "+jump"

Practice it with the preview camera

On a local server, sv_grenade_trajectory_prac_pipreview 1 shows a small camera at the landing spot while you hold a grenade. Since the November 13, 2024 update, holding jump switches that preview to the jumpthrow result, so you can see where it lands before you let go.

Pair it with sv_rethrow_last_grenade to repeat a throw without walking back. The full setup is in CS2 practice commands.

Re-enable the bind for offline practice

The restriction can be switched off with cl_allow_multi_input_binds 1. That setting needs sv_cheats 1, so it only works on a server you control, such as a local Practice game. It can't be used in matchmaking or Premier.

Offline only
sv_cheats 1
cl_allow_multi_input_binds 1
alias "+jumpthrow" "+jump;-attack"
alias "-jumpthrow" "-jump"
bind "alt" "+jumpthrow"

Hold Mouse1, then press Alt. For right-click lobs, change -attack to -attack2. Don't put this in your autoexec: on Valve servers the game ignores it, and you'll wonder why your lineups miss.

Don't try to get around the restriction with macros or keyboard tricks. Valve's August 2024 notes say players suspected of automating several actions from one input may be kicked from official matches.

Jumpthrow not working?

  • An old bind in your config. If your autoexec or config still has a +jumpthrow alias, CS2 ignores it on Valve servers. Remove it and throw by hand.
  • No grunt. You released too early or too late. Practice with the preview camera until you hear it every time.
  • The lineup was made before a patch. Valve's April 1, 2026 notes warned that “grenade lineups on sloped surfaces may have changed”, and map updates move things too. Test old lineups before relying on them.
  • Wrong throw strength. A jumpthrow with Mouse2 or both buttons lands somewhere else entirely. Check what the lineup expects.

FAQ

Is the jumpthrow bind bannable?

On Valve servers the bind simply doesn't do anything, since CS2 ignores it. Using macros or hardware tricks to automate several inputs can get you kicked, according to Valve's August 2024 notes.

Can I use a jumpthrow bind on FACEIT?

FACEIT runs its own servers and anti-cheat, and we couldn't find an official FACEIT statement on jumpthrow binds since Valve's August 2024 change. Check FACEIT's current rules before relying on one.

Do I need a jumpthrow bind for lineups?

No. Valve made jumpthrows consistent by hand, so lineups are meant to be thrown without one.

Is jump on the mouse wheel allowed?

Yes. bind "mwheeldown" "+jump" does a single action, so the multi-input restriction doesn't apply.
